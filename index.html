Fifth Column

A hidden-role WWII card game. One human seat, the rest are bots.

Play a Commandant, a Loyalist, a Saboteur or an Opportunist across the Western, Eastern and Pacific fronts, and try to work out who's who before someone works out who you are. Solo against AI opponents, with a full tutorial to learn the ropes.

Coming soon on Google Play

About

Fifth Column is a solo card game built in Godot, adapting the core rules of the San Guo Sha card game family for a WWII setting, with original maps, modes and characters on top.
